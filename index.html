Privacy Policy

Dots & Boxes Rulebreakers

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Dots & Boxes Rulebreakers is designed to be played without creating an account. The game does not request your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos, microphone, or advertising identifier.

Information stored on your device

Campaign progress, unlocks, settings, and resumable match data are saved locally on your device. This information is used only to operate the game. Removing the app or using Reset Everything may erase this local data.

Purchases

Optional purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. The game receives purchase status and product information needed to unlock or restore content, but does not receive or store your payment-card details. Store providers process information according to their own privacy policies.

Data sharing and advertising

The game does not sell personal information and does not contain third-party advertising. The game does not use analytics, behavioral tracking, or personalized advertising services.

Children’s privacy

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the game’s features or data practices change. The current version will remain available at this address.

Support and privacy questions

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the support page.

No cookies, tracking scripts, analytics, or external fonts are used on this page.
