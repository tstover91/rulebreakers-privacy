Privacy Policy

Dots & Boxes Rulebreakers / Dots & Boxes

Developer: Minor Incident Games

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy covers the Google Play phone and Automotive editions of the game. Features and data practices depend on your installed edition and version. Earlier versions without advertising or reporting do not start collecting data because this policy changes. The Automotive advertising update described below is being prepared for release.

You do not need to create a game account. The game does not request access to your contacts, photos, microphone, or precise location.

Information stored on your device

Progress, unlocks, settings, local player names, and resumable match data, where supported, are saved on your device to operate the game. Removing the app, clearing its storage, or using an available reset option may erase this information. Our custom usage events do not send local player names or the contents of your board.

Purchases

In editions offering optional purchases, the app store processes payment. The game receives purchase status and product information needed to unlock or restore content, but we do not receive your payment-card details. The Automotive-only edition is free and does not offer the full-game purchase. Store providers handle their own information under their privacy policies.

Advertising in the Automotive update

The ad-supported Automotive edition uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display banner ads on eligible setup and match-results screens. Banners are hidden during gameplay and while the app is restricted for driving. Earlier ad-free versions remain ad-free unless you install an advertising update.

When advertising services are used, Google may collect and share your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device and app information, app or ad interactions, performance and diagnostic information, and device identifiers such as an app set ID. Google uses this information to deliver and measure advertising, analyze service performance, and prevent fraud. The current Automotive integration removes the Android advertising-ID permission; this does not prevent collection of other identifiers or technical information.

We use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain and manage advertising privacy choices where required. It processes consent choices and the technical information needed to determine and present the appropriate message. Advertising requests are gated by UMP's permission to request ads.

Where offered, you can accept, decline, or manage consent in the message. You can revisit choices through Settings > Privacy & reporting > Ad privacy options when that option is required and available. Your choices can affect ad personalization and availability; declining personalized advertising does not necessarily disable all ads or all advertising-related processing. The game remains playable without agreeing to personalized advertising.

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services for details about Google's processing and privacy controls.

Optional usage and crash reporting

Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics support has been prepared for the Automotive edition but is not connected to a Firebase project as of the date above. Unconfigured builds do not send these reports. In a configured version, separate Share usage data and Share crash reports switches appear in Settings > Privacy & reporting. Both are off by default, and you can change them at any time.

If you enable usage reporting in a configured version, Google Firebase receives information such as launches, visited screens, foreground and active play time, matches started or completed, exits, game mode, board size, difficulty, player count, app/device information, and installation identifiers. We use these reports to understand play time and where players leave, and to improve the game.

If you enable crash reporting in a configured version, Firebase Crashlytics receives crash and supported app-not-responding reports, technical diagnostics, app/operating-system/device information, and installation identifiers so we can investigate failures. Our integration does not assign a player account ID or attach player names or full board contents to these reports.

These optional reporting choices are separate from advertising choices. Turning off usage or crash reporting does not disable AdMob's own measurement or diagnostics. Disabling reporting stops future collection through that reporting feature; it does not automatically erase information already received by a provider. See Firebase's privacy information.

Support, storage, and your choices

If you contact us, we receive the email address and information you voluntarily provide and use it to respond. Avoid sending sensitive information in support messages.

Local game data remains until it is removed or reset. Information processed by Google or app stores is retained according to the relevant service's policies and settings; it is not deleted simply by uninstalling the game. Google Mobile Ads and Firebase encrypt transmitted SDK data in transit. You can use the in-game controls described above, your device's privacy controls, and the providers' privacy tools to manage applicable choices.

For questions or requests about access or deletion of information we hold, email minorincidentgames@gmail.com. We will explain what information we can identify and what requests must be handled by the relevant provider. We do not require you to supply additional sensitive information merely to play.

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children. Advertising and other third-party services can process the technical information described above. A parent or guardian with a concern about a child's information can contact us at the address above.

Changes to this policy

We update this policy when features or data practices change. The current version and its update date remain available at this address.
